DUBAI MALL — LEASING GUIDE
==========================
The World's Premier Retail Destination

OVERVIEW
--------
Dubai Mall is the world's largest shopping and entertainment destination,
attracting over 105 million visitors annually. Our leasing opportunities
span 1,200+ retail outlets across 5.9 million sq ft of leasable area.

LEASING CATEGORIES
------------------
1. Luxury & Flagship (Fashion Avenue)
   - Ultra-premium brand positioning
   - Average space: 500–3,000 sqm
   - Financial terms: Premium fixed rent + revenue share
   - Lease length: 5–10 years

2. Contemporary Retail
   - Mid-tier established brands
   - Average space: 100–500 sqm
   - Financial terms: Fixed rent + service charge
   - Lease length: 3–5 years

3. Food & Beverage
   - Dining destinations
   - Average space: 200–1,000 sqm
   - Financial terms: Revenue share model
   - Lease length: 3–7 years

4. Pop-Up & Seasonal
   - Short-term activations
   - Average space: 50–300 sqm
   - Financial terms: Daily/weekly/monthly licensing
   - Duration: 1 week to 6 months
