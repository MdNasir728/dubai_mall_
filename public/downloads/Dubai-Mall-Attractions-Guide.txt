DUBAI MALL — FULL ATTRACTIONS GUIDE
====================================
The Ultimate Entertainment Destination

SIGNATURE ATTRACTIONS
---------------------

1. DUBAI AQUARIUM & UNDERWATER ZOO
   - World's largest suspended aquarium
   - 10 million litres of water
   - 33,000+ aquatic animals, 140+ species
   - Tunnel walk, cage snorkelling, shark diving

2. SKI DUBAI (adjacent)
   - Indoor real-snow ski slope
   - 5 runs including a 400m black run
   - Chairlifts, snow park, penguin encounters

3. VR PARK
   - 18,000 sqm of virtual reality experiences
   - 30+ VR/AR rides and attractions
   - Suitable for all ages

4. OLYMPIC ICE RINK
   - Olympic-sized real-ice rink
   - Public skating, figure skating, ice hockey
   - Year-round operations

5. SEGA REPUBLIC GAMING
   - 76,000 sqft gaming theme park
   - Over 150 interactive games
   - Largest gaming hub in the Middle East

6. IMAX 4DX CINEMA
   - Premium large-format screens
   - 4DX motion seating experience
   - Reel Cinemas — 22 screens total

SEASONAL & SPECIAL EVENTS
--------------------------
- Dubai Shopping Festival (January–February)
- Dubai Summer Surprises (July–August)
- Dubai Fountain shows (daily, hourly)
- Exclusive seasonal activations

THE DUBAI FOUNTAIN
------------------
The world's largest choreographed fountain system
- 275 metres long
- 22,000 gallons of water in the air at peak
- 6,600 WET Supershooters
- Visible from 27km away
